Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy explains how contact information is handled when you use Amburn’s Market’s website at amburnsmkt.com.

Information you provide

Our contact form asks for your name, email address, and message. Any other information you include in your message is also submitted. Please do not send payment card details, passwords, or other sensitive personal information through this form.

How contact information is used

Information you provide is used to receive and respond to your inquiry and handle any related follow-up. You can also contact us directly by telephone or email instead of using the form.

Formspree and email

Our contact form sends your submission to Formspree, a third-party service that processes form submissions and delivers them to us. Formspree may store submissions and collect technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, and submission time, to operate its service and prevent spam or abuse. Messages delivered to our email account are also handled by our email provider.

Formspree’s handling and retention of information are described in its privacy policy. Email sent to our Gmail address is handled under Google’s privacy policy.

Hosting and third-party content

Our website is hosted by DigitalOcean. Hosting and security services may process technical information, including IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and protect the website. The website also loads Google Maps, Google Fonts, and externally hosted website libraries. These services receive technical information when your browser connects to them and may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

See DigitalOcean’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy for information about their services.

Stored messages and your choices

Contact messages may remain in our email records and Formspree’s submission records. If you would like to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you sent us, please contact us below. Some records may need to be retained for legal or business purposes, and service providers manage their own records under their policies.
